📖 GEOSPATIAL & REMOTE SENSING TERMINOLOGY GLOSSARY
SAR RADAR SENSOR
Synthetic Aperture Radar: Radar-based imaging that pierces through dense clouds, smoke, and darkness to capture terrain structure 24/7.
LULC ANALYTICS
Land Use / Land Cover: Automatic classification of satellite pixels into vegetation, urban buildings, water bodies, and bare soil.
NIR OPTICAL BAND
Near-Infrared Light: Wavelength just beyond visible red light, heavily reflected by healthy chlorophyll in plants to detect vegetation health.
NDVI SPECTRAL INDEX
Normalized Difference Vegetation Index: A mathematical formula measuring greenery density and crop health from satellite bands.
GeoTIFF DATA FORMAT
Geographic TIFF Image: A high-resolution satellite image file embedded with real-world spatial coordinates and map projections.
MGRS NAVIGATION
Military Grid Reference System: Standardized alpha-numeric global grid system used by defense forces for pinpoint target location.
VLM AI CORE
Vision-Language Model: AI neural network (like Gemini 3.6 Flash) capable of understanding satellite images and text queries simultaneously.
SWIR INFRARED BAND
Short-Wave Infrared: Wavelength used to detect moisture levels, wildfire hotspots, soil composition, and urban concrete.
Grounding AI DETECTION
Visual Object Grounding: Locating specific targets (runways, vessels, solar panels) mentioned in a text query and drawing bounding boxes.
Bi-Temporal CHANGE DETECT
Time-Series Comparison: Analyzing two satellite photos of the exact same area taken at different dates ($T_1$ vs $T_2$) to detect changes.
